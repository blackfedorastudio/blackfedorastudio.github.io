Space Christmas Ideas

Want a Christmas that is a little different this year? Here are our favourite ways to mix the holidays with rockets, planets and a retro space-age look, from decorations to handmade gifts.

1. Decorate the tree with planets

Swap a few classic baubles for planets. A ringed Saturn, a blue Earth and a red Mars look great next to gold stars. Finish with a star topper and warm white lights, and the whole tree becomes a little solar system.

2. Hang retro space Christmas posters

Printable posters are the fastest way to change the feel of a room for the holidays. Frame two or three on a shelf or hallway wall, then swap them back after New Year. Bold titles like Ho Ho Houston, Silent Night In Orbit or Deck The Planets are a fun conversation starter, especially in a kids room.

3. Make your own Christmas cards

Handmade cards always mean more. Print clipart on card stock, add a short message and you are done:

  • A rocket sleigh with "Merry Christmas from outer space"
  • A snowman astronaut with "Have a cool Christmas"
  • A moon in a Santa hat with "Silent night, starry night"

Transparent PNG clipart works in Canva, Word, Procreate or any card template, and you can use the same pieces for gift tags and envelope seals.

4. Cricut shirts for the whole family

Matching Christmas shirts are a classic, and a space theme makes them stand out in the family photo. Single-layer SVG cut files are the easiest to weed. Use white vinyl on navy or black shirts and black vinyl on white or cream shirts.

5. Personalised Christmas mugs

A sublimation mug with a funny space design is a gift people actually use every day. Print the wrap at 100% scale, wrap it around a white sublimation mug and press it following your mug and press instructions.

6. A space-themed advent idea

Number 24 small envelopes, add a space fact and a tiny treat to each one, and hang them in a line like a rocket countdown. On the 24th, the last envelope says "Lift off!".

7. Space gifts for every age

  • Kids: a space coloring book, glow-in-the-dark stars, a rocket night light.
  • Teens: a retro space poster set for their room.
  • Adults: a 2027 retro space wall calendar with every moon phase, or a digital planner for the new year.
  • Crafters: a clipart or SVG bundle to make their own gifts.
