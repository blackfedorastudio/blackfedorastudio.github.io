About the studio

Black Fedora Studio

Black Fedora Studio is a small independent design studio, selling on Etsy since November 2026. We make printable calendars, digital planners, wall art, party printables, Christmas designs, clipart, SVG cut files and brushes for creators.

Questions

FAQ

How do I get my files after I buy?

Etsy gives you a download link right after payment. You can also find your files any time under Purchases in your Etsy account.

Will I receive a physical product?

No. Every product is an instant digital download. You print it at home or at a print shop, or use it in your favourite app.

Which file should I print for my frame?

Our posters come in 5 ratios: 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11x14 and ISO A sizes. Pick the one that matches your frame. Our printing guide explains it step by step.

Does the digital planner work on Android?

Yes. It is a hyperlinked PDF that works in GoodNotes and Notability on iPad, and in PDF apps like Xodo on Android tablets.

Can I use the clipart and SVG files for products I sell?

Yes. Our clipart, SVG and mug designs include commercial use on physical products, up to 500 sales per design. You cannot resell or share the digital files themselves.

Where can I buy your products?

All our products are sold through our Etsy shop, Black Fedora Studio, with secure checkout and instant download.
